# AI Fluency — Notes

## 1. Introduction to AI Fluency

**Course:** [AI Fluency: Framework and foundations](https://academy.claude.com/courses/ai-fluency-framework-foundations)

### What is AI Fluency?

AI Fluency is not primarily about understanding how AI technology works.

It is about being able to **collaborate with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely**.

The important shift is from:

> "How does AI work?"

to:

> "How do I work effectively with AI?"

The technology will continue to change, so the goal is to develop skills and principles that remain useful even as individual AI tools and models evolve.

### The 4D Framework

The course introduces four core competencies for working with AI:

1. **Delegation** - deciding what to delegate to AI and how
2. **Description** - communicating what I want from AI
3. **Discernment** - evaluating AI's outputs and behaviour
4. **Diligence** - taking responsibility for AI-assisted work

These four competencies form the foundation of the course.

### Important distinction

AI collaboration is not simply:

```text
Ask AI > Receive answer > Done
```

It is a collaborative process where I need to:

1. Decide what AI should do
2. Describe what I need
3. Evaluate what AI produces
4. Take responsibility for the result

The last two points are especially important: using AI does not transfer responsibility for the outcome to the AI.

### Key takeaway

> **AI fluency is a skill of collaboration, not simply a skill of using an AI tool.**

The goal is to develop a durable way of working with AI rather than collecting temporary prompting tricks.
