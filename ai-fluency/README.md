* **Completion:** 4 hours

## Topics

The course introduces the **AI Fluency Framework**, built around four core competencies:

* **Delegation** — deciding what to delegate to AI and how
* **Description** — communicating what you want from AI
* **Discernment** — evaluating AI outputs, processes, and behavior
* **Diligence** — taking responsibility for AI-assisted work

It also covers:

* Generative AI fundamentals
* AI capabilities and limitations
* Automation, Augmentation, and Agency
* Effective prompting techniques
* The Description–Discernment loop
* Responsible AI collaboration
