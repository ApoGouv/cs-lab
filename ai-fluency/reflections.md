# AI Fluency — Reflections

## Introduction to AI Fluency

### What challenges have I encountered when working with AI to achieve specific outcomes?

For starters, I find it difficult to find the right balance between giving AI enough context for a given problem and not overwhelming it with too much information.

Another challenge is knowing when the answer is actually correct. I have seen AI struggle to keep the logic of a specific version of a given CMS or e-commerce platform, e.g. OpenCart 3.x. It can easily introduce mistakes and other errors.

In addition, and maybe related to the first challenge, I've noticed that the quality of the result depends heavily on how well and clearly the problem, the constraints, and the expected outcome are defined.

### What possibilities for AI collaboration excite me most?

Using it as a code buddy! We exchange ideas, it helps me find bugs that would slip into my first iteration, and we can explore architectures that I haven't had the chance to work with yet.

So, exploring unknown areas with AI's help is definitely useful, although it clearly needs caution!

Learning new topics, summarizing notes, and getting new perspectives when working on a problem are some other things I like when working with AI.

### What do I hope to gain from this course?

Probably, to develop a better mindset for working with AI.

And to apply what I learn with any AI provider, whether OpenAI, Claude, Copilot, or any other out there.

At the end, I would like AI to help me learn faster, improve my coding skills, and become a better developer.
